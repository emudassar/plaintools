# 10-tool shortlist: untapped corners sweep, 2026-10-14

A shortlist, not research files. Each pick still needs its own `research/<slug>.md` (from
`_TEMPLATE.md`) with the source verified **before** `/add-tool`. Nothing here was built.
None of these overlap the 62 live tools or the 2026-10-04 shortlist.

## How this was found (all real data, pulled 2026-10-14)

1. **Semrush Keyword Magic (US db)** via balochseotools (sr01). 220 seeds aimed at corners the
   last sweep skipped: hobbies/crafts (knitting, quilting, soap, candles, pottery, sourdough),
   marine/outboards, winter sports sizing, diving, archery, reloading, machining, homestead
   animals, heating fuel, collectibles, and "age / year by serial number", "date code", "decoder",
   "identification chart" patterns. Filter KD 0–14, volume ≥ 100 → **7,457 keywords**; 1,129 were
   tool-shaped (calculator / chart / lookup / decoder / how many …). A second pull (KD ≤ 30) sized
   the clusters around the winners.
2. **Top-10 SERP check for ~100 candidates** (Semrush page AS / referring domains).
   "Strong" = page AS ≥ 25, or ≥ 25 ref domains, or a big calculator/retail brand.
   **S = number of strong pages in the top 10.**

Format: **volume/mo · KD · S** — notable ranking pages as `domain(pageAS/refdomains)`.
